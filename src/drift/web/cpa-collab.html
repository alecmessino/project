For Professionals

For CPAs and estate attorneys.

We don't replace you. We sequence the work so your advice lands in the right order — and we keep the household's open matters in one register you can see.

How we work together

You stay the tax and legal advisor. Driftwood coordinates the household's decisions across your advice, the investment plan, and the estate documents — so a recommendation you make isn't undermined by a move made elsewhere a month later.

One shared register. The client's open matters — funding, timing, titling, withdrawals — live in a standing Opportunity Register with an owner and a date. You see the same picture the client sees.

A coordinated calendar. When a sale, a Roth window, or a trust funding depends on your input, we bring it to you in time, not after the window has shut.
