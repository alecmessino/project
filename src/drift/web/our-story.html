Our Firm

A practice built to coordinate, not to manage.

Driftwood exists for one gap: the space between your investment advisor, your CPA, and your estate attorney, where decisions quietly diverge. We are the person holding all three to the same plan.

The gap we fill

Most households already have good professionals. What they lack is coordination — someone reading the investment, the tax, the estate, and the cash flow as one system and putting an owner against every open decision. That is the entire practice. We do not replace your advisor, CPA, or attorney; we make their work land in the right order.

How we are paid

Driftwood charges to coordinate decisions, not to manage a number. The engagement fee is defined in writing before any work begins, and scoped to the household — not to assets under management. How we are paid, and why, is set out in full on Fees.
