VII

Appendix

Structure
Every issue runs the same seven divisions in the same order. A division may be thin in a given quarter; it is not reordered, and it is not dropped.
Survey plates
The artwork is a closed library of ten structural plates. A piece is assigned the plate whose structure matches its subject. The plates carry no data: they are not charts, they encode no measurement, and nothing in them should be read as a figure.
Figures
This issue publishes no dollar or percentage figure. Where a piece links to one, the figure lives on the exhibit that derives it, alongside its provenance.
Model portfolio
References to the Model Portfolio describe a hypothetical, append-only backtest — not actual trading and not any client account. Past performance, hypothetical or actual, does not guarantee future results.
Corrections
Corrections are published in the following issue's Appendix and the original entry is annotated rather than rewritten.
