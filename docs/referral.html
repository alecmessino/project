Making a referral

What happens after you make the introduction.

Four steps. You are told where it stands at each one, and your client is never routed away from you. If coordination is not the problem, the referral closes and we tell you why.

The terms
What we will not do.

Take work that is yours.

We do not file returns, draft or amend documents, or offer your client a second opinion on your work.

Route your client around you.

Your engagement stays yours. Where a decision is yours to make, the register names you as its owner.

Pay for the introduction.

Driftwood does not pay or receive compensation for professional referrals. Your reason to refer is the outcome, and nothing else.

No client information is collected on this page. Any exchange of client data happens only with your client's written consent, and through the channel their engagement specifies.
