IV

Decision Memos

The reasoning of record on one decision already taken — dated, attributed, with the alternatives that were rejected and the condition that would reopen it. The Library answers what happens when; a memo shows what was thought, and when.

The order was the decisionMemo A family intended to sell a business and to leave the state. Both were settled; the sequence was not, and the sequence is what the outcome turned on. Reducing a concentrated positionMemo The same form applied to a portfolio decision: how fast to exit, at what tax cost, and into what — with the hedging route weighed and rejected. The Decision RegisterIndex Every structural decision on the day it was made, and the trigger that reopens it. Append-only: nothing deleted, only superseded.
