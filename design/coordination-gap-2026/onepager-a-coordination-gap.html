Illinois does not have portability, and the gap widens every year.

The federal exemption is $15,000,000 per person for 2026, made permanent by the July 2025 tax act and indexed to inflation from 2027 forward. The Illinois exclusion is $4,000,000, has never been indexed, and has not moved in more than a decade. HB2601 would have raised it to $8,000,000; it was re-referred to the House Rules Committee in March 2025 and has not passed. Every year the federal number rises, the distance between the two grows.

"The portability and carry-over of the unused federal exemption to the surviving spouse is inapplicable to the computation and assessment of the Illinois Estate Tax."

If the first spouse dies without planning, that $4,000,000 exclusion is not carried over. It is gone.

$13,610,000 → $0

The fix is in the same document as the problem, and it has a nine-month clock on it.

The Attorney General's fact sheet also works an estate of $13,610,000, with a surviving spouse and an Illinois QTIP election of $9,610,000, to zero Illinois estate tax. Illinois has permitted a state-only QTIP election for deaths on or after January 1, 2009, separate and independent from any federal election.

It must be made on a timely filed Form 700, due nine months after death, with the election box checked and the property scheduled. Nothing about it is automatic. It requires someone who is watching for it while the documents, the balance sheet, and the return are all still in motion.

Inherited retirement accounts
10 years

The stretch IRA is gone. Most non-spouse heirs must empty an inherited retirement account within ten years of the owner's death, and where the owner had already begun required distributions, take them in years one through nine as well. The waivers covering 2021 through 2024 have expired. A shortfall carries a 25 percent excise tax.

The distributions land as ordinary income, usually in the heir's highest-earning decade. Whether to convert during the owner's lifetime is a portfolio question, a tax question, and an estate question at once. It is normally asked by nobody.

What the vehicle costs, before fees
1.90% / 0.70%

Over the ten years ended December 2024, taxes consumed an average of 1.90 percent of return annually for U.S. large-cap equity mutual funds and 0.70 percent for equity ETFs. On $100,000 that difference left the ETF investor with more than $30,000 in additional value.

In 2024, over 80 percent of U.S. equity mutual funds distributed capital gains, against 5 percent of ETFs. For many funds the tax cost exceeds the expense ratio, and almost everyone shops the expense ratio.

The documents themselves
46%

Among households holding $3 million or more in investable assets, 46 percent have all three of a will, a living will or advance directive, and a durable power of attorney. Fifty-five percent have a trust, and a third say they understand trusts well.

Among the business owners in that group, 78 percent call succession planning important and 20 percent have a fully documented plan. The intent is nearly universal. The execution is where it stops.
