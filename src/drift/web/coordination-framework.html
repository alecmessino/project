Coordination

The framework: seven systems, one plan.

Every household runs on the same seven systems. Coordination is what keeps them pointing the same direction — and what most households are missing.

The seven systems
InvestmentsWhat is owned, how it is built and held, and at what turnover.
TaxesWhere return is lost to tax, and how gains, losses, and brackets are timed.
Cash FlowThe reserve, the liquidity for life, and the buffer against forced selling.
Estate & LegacyWho inherits, how, and whether the documents own what they claim.
Risk & ProtectionWhat a single decision could expose, and how it is contained.
Business OwnershipConcentration, exit, and how the business connects to everything else.
Family / PurposeThe why the plan exists — and the decisions it should serve.
No decision touches just one system.
